ProteomIQon - LabeledProteinQuantification

Tool Description

LabeledProteinQuantification takes the .quantAndProt rows from JoinQuantPepIonsWithProteins and aggregates them to one light intensity, one heavy intensity and one ratio per protein group and run.

What's happening?

In a 15N metabolic labeling experiment one sample grows on 15N, is mixed with the 14N sample, and both versions of every peptide are measured in the same run, so the ratio of the light and the heavy peak is a direct comparison of the two samples. How the label enters the search is described in BioFSharp.Mz, and how the ratio is formed from the two peaks in the quantification chapter. It works in up to four steps. The first step is mandatory: every row already holds a light and a heavy intensity, the step divides them into Ratio_LightByHeavy and merges the rows identified from the light and from the heavy form of the same peptide ion. The second step merges charge states and the third merges modified forms of a peptide, both optional. The fourth merges all peptides of a protein group. Every step transforms, filters and aggregates light, heavy and ratio separately.
